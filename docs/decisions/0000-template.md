# ADR-NNNN: short decision title

- status: proposed
- date: YYYY-MM-DD
- deciders: team or names

## context

the forces at play: requirements, constraints, and what made this a real
choice rather than a default. describe the problem, not the solution. a reader
who disagrees with the decision should still agree this section states the
situation fairly.

## decisions

what was decided. name the chosen option, then name each rejected alternative
with the one reason it lost. if the decision has several parts under one theme,
break them into `###` sub-headings:

### <sub-decision, if any>

chosen: X over Y and Z. Y rejected because <one reason>. Z rejected because
<one reason>.

## consequences

what this commits us to. positive and negative both:

- what gets easier
- what gets harder or slower
- new failure modes or operational burden it introduces
- follow-up work it creates or unblocks
